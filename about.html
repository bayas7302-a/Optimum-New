About Us

Delivering accurate and secure background screening

Optimum for Professional Services was established in 2020 to support government entities, regulatory authorities, and organizations operating in regulated and high-impact environments. Founded to address the growing need for accurate, secure, and reliable verification of academic, professional, and employment credentials, Optimum for Professional Services has evolved into a comprehensive provider of background screening, document verification, and workforce compliance solutions.

Headquartered in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and expanding across the GCC and international markets

Optimum for Professional Services works closely with government entities, professional councils, licensing bodies, and regulatory authorities responsible for workforce accreditation, professional classification, and compliance oversight. Through this collaboration, we contribute to strengthening governance frameworks, reducing employment and licensing risks, and mitigating fraud, misrepresentation, and compliance gaps across critical sectors.

Optimum for Professional Services is committed to setting a new standard for verification quality — ensuring that every client receives accurate, verified, and decision-ready information.

What We Do

Beyond core background screening and document verification, Optimum for Professional Services delivers an integrated portfolio of strategic solutions designed to support workforce readiness, regulatory compliance, and institutional performance. These include:

Education Verification

Direct confirmation of academic degrees and transcripts globally

Identity Verification

Rigorous authentication of government-issued identity documents

Employment Verification

Thorough validation of past employment history and periods of employment

Reference Checks

Structured interviews to assess performance and cultural fit

Professional Licence and Certificate Verification

Authentication of specialised credentials through relevant regulatory bodies

Criminal Records Database Search

Extensive searching of global and regional criminal-record databases

Police Clearance Verification

Verification of official police-clearance certificates

Watchlist Monitoring

Continuous monitoring against global watchlists, sanctions lists, and politically exposed persons (PEPs)

Social Media Screening

Compliance-focused analysis of public digital footprints

This expanded capability enables Optimum for Professional Services to serve not only as a verification provider but also as a long-term strategic partner, supporting organizations in building sustainable regulatory and operational frameworks.

Our vision

To be the global leader in trusted people intelligence and professional assessment, enabling organizations to understand the complete history, qualifications, experience, and competencies of individuals and make fair, confident, and compliant decisions.

Our Mission

Optimum for Professional Services provides trusted verification, credential, experience, and competency assessment services, alongside evidence-based framework design and advisory support, helping governments and organizations reduce risk and make informed, compliant decisions.

Our Verification Process

At Optimum for Professional Services, our added value lies in delivering a seamless blend of accuracy, speed, and global capability guided by integrity, transparency, and operational excellence.

  1. Direct Source Validation

    We confirm education, employment, licenses, identity, and other records directly from issuing authorities around the world.

  2. Technology-Enabled Screening

    Digital tools, global databases, and automated risk engines ensure fast, secure, and highly accurate results.

  3. Compliance and Governance

    Our processes follow international verification standards and fully support Saudi regulatory expectations for transparency and reliability.

  4. Clear and Secure Reporting

    Reports are delivered in a structured, traceable format that enables confident decision-making.

  5. Comprehensive Coverage

    From identity checks to criminal records and global sanctions, Optimum for Professional Services provides a complete screening ecosystem tailored to the needs of national institutions.
