Overview

Support Safer Hiring and Regulatory Compliance

Optimum for Professional Services conducts criminal background checks through authorized databases and official sources, in full compliance with local laws and data protection regulations.

Scope Of Checks

Jurisdiction-specific criminal record searches

We conduct checks through the appropriate channels for each relevant jurisdiction, since criminal record availability, scope, and access procedures vary by country and region.

Compliance with consent and legal requirements

All checks are carried out only with the necessary candidate consent and in accordance with applicable local laws governing background screening.

Expiration status

Criminal record data is treated as highly sensitive and is collected, stored, and transmitted using secure, access-controlled processes throughout the verification.

Why It’s Critical

Criminal records checks help protect workplaces, safeguard vulnerable populations, and ensure compliance with statutory and regulatory obligations.

Who Uses This Service

  • Employers across regulated sectors
  • Government and public institutions
  • Licensing and regulatory authorities
